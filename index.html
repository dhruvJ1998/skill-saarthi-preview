Questions, answered

Before you join.

Here is the essential information for becoming a Skill Saarthi. The complete programme terms apply to every referral and benefit.

Who can become a Skill Saarthi?

School leaders, teachers, counsellors, alumni, parents, professionals, education consultants and community connectors may apply, subject to the programme terms.

How is a referral attributed?

Use your unique referral link or code. The first verified referral and the official records determine attribution if more than one person refers the same student.

When are benefits released?

Eligible benefits are released in six semester-wise parts, subject to the student's continuity, applicable fee status and other programme conditions.

What happens if an admission is cancelled or discontinued?

Any unpaid future benefits may not become payable. Refer to the programme terms for the complete eligibility, cancellation, tax and KYC requirements.
